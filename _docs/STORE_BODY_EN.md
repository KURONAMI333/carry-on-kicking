Kick the mobs you pick up with [Carry On](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/carry-on). Hold right-click to charge, then release to send them flying. Longer charges give stronger kicks, reaching full power after one second.

1. Pick up a mob using Carry On's usual controls, then release the pickup button.
2. Aim where you want to kick it and hold right-click to wind up.
3. Release to kick. A quick tap under 0.2 seconds simply drops the mob.

Your left leg winds up and swings as you kick, with a rising charge sound and an impact sound on release. You can hold a full charge until you are ready. Opening a menu while charging cancels the kick and keeps the mob in your hands.

The addon works with mobs that Carry On lets you pick up. Blocks and carried players keep their usual Carry On controls. No new items or crafting recipes are needed.
